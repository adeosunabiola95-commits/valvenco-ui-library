Palette

Grey is the quieter button. The page stays white. Body copy is black at 80%, and descriptions sit at 60%.

  • Primary #00B1F5 · --c-primary
  • White #FFFFFF · --c-white

  • Black #000000 · --c-black

Usage

Each ground keeps one job. The label color is part of the rule.

  • Primary

    Main action. The label is white.

  • White

    Page and stages. Headings are black, body is 80%.

  • Grey

    Quieter button and the invert fill. The label is black.

  • Black

    The ground for a white button. Type on it is white.
